Entry 1: Form Design

Though these areas of design are seemingly simple, they're what make or break the usability and quality of a website. There have been many a time in which I decide to use a different website for the task I hope to do because the text was unreadable or formatted strangely, and that lends itself to a bad image for the company. If the design of their website is not up to par, I tend not to expect their product or service to be [that is, though, if we're talking about a larger corporation and not a small business— I'll usualy give them the benefit of the doubt].

While perhaps a controversial take, an example I enjoyed for its wackiness is Pit Viper's website. The design looks like something from 15 years ago, and there isn't the clean aesthetic most websites have now, but they're unique design style comes through very strongly without inhibiting user experience at all, and I find that admirable.
