Entry 1: Form Design

Though these areas of design seem simple, they're what make or break the usability and quality of a website. I've often decided to switch to a different website for the task I'd hoped to do because the text was unreadable or formatted strangely, and that lends itself to a bad image for the company. If the design of their website is not up to par, I tend not to expect their product or service to be [when concerning larger corporation and not a small business— I'll usualy give them the benefit of the doubt].

While perhaps a strange take, I enjoy Pit Viper for the wackiness of its website. The design looks like something from 15 years ago and there isn't the clean aesthetic most websites use now, but their unique design style comes through very strongly without inhibiting user — and I find that admirable.
